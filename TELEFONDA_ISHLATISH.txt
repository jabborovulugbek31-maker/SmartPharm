SMARTPHARM — TELEFONDA ISHLATISH

1) Kompyuter va telefonni BIR XIL Wi‑Fi ga ulang.
2) SmartPharm papkasida CMD oching.
3) python app.py buyrug'ini ishga tushiring.
4) Kompyuterning IPv4 manzilini bilish uchun CMD ga ipconfig yozing.
5) "IPv4 Address" qatoridagi manzilni toping, masalan 192.168.1.7.
6) Telefonda brauzerni ochib http://192.168.1.7:5000 kabi manzilni yozing.
7) SmartPharm telefon ekraniga mos ochiladi.
8) Chrome/Safari menyusidan "Add to Home Screen / На главный экран" orqali bosh ekranga qo'shish mumkin (PWA imkoniyati brauzer va tarmoq xavfsizligiga bog'liq).

Agar telefon ulanmasa:
- Windows Firewall ruxsat so'rasa, Private networks uchun ruxsat bering.
- Kompyuter va telefon bir xil Wi‑Fi ekanini tekshiring.
- CMD oynasini yopmang; python app.py ishlayotgan bo'lishi kerak.
